Privacy policy

How Horoscope Daily handles your information.

Publisher: SMC Developer
Effective date: 2026-11-05

Premium subscriptions

When subscription services are configured, RevenueCat and your app store process an anonymous app user identifier, purchase and subscription records, and technical information to verify and restore Premium access. Your name, birthday and zodiac profile are not sent to RevenueCat by this integration. Payment details are handled by the store, not by this app. Resetting local app data does not cancel your subscription or delete store/RevenueCat purchase records. Manage cancellation in your store subscription settings. Contact us for questions about purchase-related data. RevenueCat also provides purchase reporting and analytics to the publisher.

Remote push notifications

If you enable push notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) and Apple Push Notification service on iOS process installation identifiers, messaging registration information and technical connection data to deliver messages to your device. Your saved name, birthday and zodiac profile are not sent by this integration. You can disable push notifications in Settings; the app then unregisters this installation from messaging. Local scheduled reminders remain separate. Reset data also disables push notifications. Processing by Google and Apple is subject to their respective privacy policies.

Notification inbox

Notifications received by the app are saved on your device with their title, message, notification identifier, date, reading destination and read/unread status. This history supports the notification inbox and unread badges; it is not synchronized to an app account or a separate inbox server. Records remain until you reset app data. Some notifications shown by your operating system may be saved only when opened. Turning off push notifications does not erase existing inbox records.

Information kept on your device

Your optional profile name, birthday, zodiac sign, saved signs and saved compatibility pairs (both custom names and zodiac signs) are stored locally, alongside theme, language, reminder preferences and cached content. Your 18-or-older confirmation, accepted Terms version and acceptance date are stored locally. Terms agreement does not grant permission for notifications or optional data processing. No identity document or account is required. Use a nickname for another person unless you have permission to use their name. These profile fields are not sent to RevenueCat or Firebase by this integration.

Internet requests and external services

The app downloads public horoscope content and images. Your saved name, birthday and selected zodiac sign are not included in these requests. GitHub Pages and external image hosts, including Google-hosted image services and other illustration hosts, receive connection information such as your IP address, request time and technical request details, and may keep logs under their own policies. This version has no advertising SDK or separate app-usage analytics SDK and does not sell your saved profile. RevenueCat purchase reporting is described above.

Reminders and permissions

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device only when you enable them and allow notifications. They use Cambodia time (Asia/Phnom_Penh); this does not require your GPS location. You can turn reminders off in Settings or revoke notification permission in your phone settings.

Sharing

Share app sends a public website/store link through the system share sheet. Horoscope cards can include your saved profile name. Compatibility cards can include both saved pair names below their signs. The preview shows what will be exported; switch off Show my name or Show pair names to omit names. Names are included by default when available. You choose whether to send the image and who receives it. The recipient or service may retain copies. Copy actions place text on your clipboard. You can also save the preview to your photo gallery. Permission is requested only when saving; the app does not read your photo library. Saved images may include visible names and remain in Photos after app reset.

Retention and deleting local data

Saved profiles, pairs, age confirmation, Terms acceptance record and preferences remain on this installation until edited, deleted or reset. Delete individual pairs from Saved pairs. Settings > Reset data cancels reminders, disables push, clears these local details and cached content, and returns to setup. Public content may download again. Reset does not cancel subscriptions or delete purchase records held by stores/RevenueCat, host logs, recipients’ shared copies or operating-system backups. Contact the publisher about data requests; third-party retention also follows their policies.

Adults only and privacy requests

This app is intended only for adults aged 18 and older. Do not use it if you are under 18. Contact the publisher if you believe a child’s information was submitted to an external service. You may contact us to request information, access, correction or deletion, or exercise other rights available under applicable law. Services used by the app may process data outside your country; their privacy policies describe their safeguards and retention. No universal retention period is promised for independent service providers.

Optional app integrity checks

If App Check is enabled in a release, Google Play Integrity or Apple App Attest/DeviceCheck processes app/device integrity information to assess authenticity. This feature does not send your saved profile names or birthdays. The relevant Google/Apple service privacy policies apply.

Policy updates

This policy describes the current version of Horoscope Daily. If the app adds features that change how information is handled, SMC Developer will update this policy and its effective date.
