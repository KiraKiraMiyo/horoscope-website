Privacy policy

How Khmer Horoscope handles your information.

Publisher: SMC Developer
Effective date: 2026-10-17

Information kept on your device

If you choose to enter a name, birthday or zodiac sign, the app stores it on your device to personalize your greeting and horoscope. Language, theme, reminder preferences and cached horoscope content are also stored locally. A birthday is optional. You do not need to create an account.

Internet requests and external services

The app downloads public horoscope content and images. Your saved name, birthday and selected zodiac sign are not included in these requests. GitHub Pages and external image hosts, including Google-hosted image services and other illustration hosts, receive connection information such as your IP address, request time and technical request details, and may keep logs under their own policies. The app has no advertising or analytics SDKs in this version and does not sell your saved profile.

Reminders and permissions

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device only when you enable them and allow notifications. They use Cambodia time (Asia/Phnom_Penh); this does not require your GPS location. You can turn reminders off in Settings or revoke notification permission in your phone settings.

Sharing

Share app opens the system share sheet with the app name, a short description and a public website or store link. It does not automatically include your saved name, birthday or profile. You choose whether to send anything and which app or person receives it. The receiving service then handles that content under its own policy. Copy actions put the displayed text on the clipboard.

Retention and deleting local data

Your saved profile and preferences remain on this installation until you change or reset them. Settings > Reset data cancels app reminders, clears saved profile details and preferences, removes cached horoscope and image data, and returns you to setup. Public content can be downloaded again when the app is used. Reset does not delete logs held by external hosts or copies retained in backups managed by your operating system. Backup and restore behavior depends on your device settings.

Policy updates

This policy describes the current version of Khmer Horoscope. If the app adds features that change how information is handled, SMC Developer will update this policy and its effective date.
